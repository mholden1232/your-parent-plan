Your Parent Plan

Care Options

Compare levels of care, true facility costs and what the parent can realistically afford.

Independent living

Mostly independent, with community, meals, transportation and less home upkeep.

Assisted living

Housing plus help with everyday activities, medication support and personal care.

Memory care

Secure, dementia-focused support when cognition, wandering, safety or supervision needs increase.

When should we consider more help?

Falls or mobility concerns
Medication mistakes
Meals / weight loss / unsafe cooking
Difficulty bathing, dressing or toileting
Driving concerns
Missed bills, scams or money-management problems
Memory, wandering or nighttime confusion
Family can no longer safely provide enough help

This is a planning discussion tool, not a diagnosis or recommendation of a specific level of care.
