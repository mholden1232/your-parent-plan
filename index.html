What’s inside

Work through it a little at a time. The plan keeps track of what you’ve done and points you to what’s next.

  1. My Plan

    What’s complete, what still needs attention, and your next three things.

  2. Essentials

    Emergency contacts, legal documents, insurance, and the people who matter.

  3. Money Snapshot

    Income, bills, and savings, and what that means for what your parent can afford.

  4. Care Options

    The real monthly cost of each community, with your notes beside the numbers.

  5. Scan & Organize

    Bring paperwork in without retyping it.

Questions it helps you answer

Can Mom afford assisted living?
Compare a community’s real monthly cost against her income and savings, and see how long the savings would last.
Where is the power of attorney?
Keep legal, insurance, and emergency information together, so nobody is digging through drawers in a hurry.
What are we forgetting?
The checklist shows the gaps while they’re still small.
Which community is actually better?
Line up costs, pros, and cons side by side instead of from memory.
