Privacy & Security — Prototype vs. Real App

This prototype is not secure storage. It has no user authentication, no production encryption guarantees, no family permission system, and no audit history. Do not enter real banking credentials, Social Security numbers, passwords, or other highly sensitive information here.

Production requirement: encrypted storage and transport, secure sign-in, role-based family permissions, audit history, backups/recovery, secure document handling, and a formal privacy/security review before anyone stores real family financial, legal, insurance or health information.

The product should store bank/investment references such as institution, account type and last four digits where useful — not raw online-banking passwords.

Scan Inbox — What the App Will Do

Scan Inbox

Goal: almost no retyping. Every scan lands here first so the family can review what the app found before it changes the parent's record.

Pill bottle

Suggest medication name, strength, directions, prescriber, pharmacy, and refill count exactly as printed on the label → user confirms → Important Health Information.

Insurance card

Suggest carrier, plan/member identifiers and phone numbers → user confirms → Insurance section. The production app should protect this as sensitive information.

Discharge / visit paperwork

Suggest diagnoses, medication changes, follow-up appointments and instructions → user confirms → Medical timeline + calendar + next steps.

Legal document

Recognize likely document type, names, dates and document location → user confirms → Family Vault. The app should not pretend to interpret legal validity.

Facility quote / price sheet

Suggest room & board, care-level fees, medication fees, transportation, meals and other charges → user confirms → Facility Calculator.

Bill / EOB

Suggest provider, amount, due date and insurance/payment status → user confirms → Financial tracker + reminder.

Living Options
Quick definitions: Assisted living commonly includes housing plus services such as meals, personal-care help, medication help, housekeeping/laundry, supervision and activities, but exactly what is included varies by community. Independent living generally provides fewer hands-on care services. Memory care adds a secure, dementia-focused care environment and often carries a higher price.

Where Should Mom or Dad Live?

A diagnosis alone does not determine the right setting. Think about function, safety, supervision, preferences, finances and what the community can actually provide.

Can they bathe, dress, toilet and eat safely?

Is nighttime supervision becoming necessary?
Can the current setting meet their needs consistently?

1. Community type & levels of care

Is this independent living, assisted living, memory care, skilled nursing — or a campus with several levels?
Can a resident move from independent → assisted → memory care without leaving the campus?
Who decides when a higher level of care is required?
Is a room guaranteed at the next level, or is there a waitlist?
Is there a new entrance fee, deposit, assessment, or contract when care level changes?

2. Room & board

What is the monthly room charge?
What room sizes / layouts are available and what does each cost?
Are utilities included?
Is Wi-Fi / cable included?
Is housekeeping included? How often?
Is laundry included? Personal laundry too?
Is there a one-time community / entrance / move-in fee?
What is the refundable / non-refundable policy?

3. Meals & food

How many meals per day are included?
Are snacks included?
Can residents choose meal times or dining venues?
Can they accommodate diabetic, low-sodium, texture-modified, allergy or other special diets?
Can meals be delivered to the room? Is there a fee?
Can family members eat with the resident? What does it cost?
Can we eat a meal here before deciding?

4. Personal care & assistance

What help with bathing, dressing, toileting, grooming and mobility is included?
How are care levels priced — flat fee, tiers, or per service?
What causes the care level / monthly fee to increase?
How often are care needs reassessed?
Can two-person transfers or mobility assistance be provided?
What incontinence supplies / help are included and what costs extra?

5. Medication management

Is medication management included or an extra monthly fee?
Who orders, stores and administers medications?
Can the resident keep using their current pharmacy?
Is there a required pharmacy or medication-packaging service?
What happens after a medication change or hospital discharge?
Who communicates with doctors about refills, side effects or medication problems?

6. Medical care & insurance coordination

Is a nurse on site? What hours? Overnight?
Are physicians, nurse practitioners, therapy, podiatry, dental or other clinicians available on site?
Does the community coordinate appointments and medical records?
Will staff communicate with Medicare / health insurance for covered medical services, or is that the family's responsibility?
Which medical services are billed separately to insurance?
Which services are private-pay and not part of room / care fees?
Can outside home-health, therapy or hospice agencies come in?
How are hospital transfers handled? Who goes with the resident? Who calls the family?

7. Transportation

Is transportation included?
Is it only for group outings, or also individual medical appointments?
How far will transportation go?
How much advance notice is required?
Is wheelchair-accessible transportation available?
Are there additional transportation fees?

8. What is actually in the building?

Dining room / café / private dining

Movie / activity / game rooms

9. Activities, social life & day-to-day living

What activities happen every day, evenings and weekends?
How are new residents introduced to people and activities?
What happens if a resident becomes isolated or stops attending meals?
Are pets allowed?
Can residents bring their own furniture?
Are overnight guests permitted?
What are visiting-hour rules?

10. Staffing & safety

How many direct-care staff are on duty during the day?
How many are on duty overnight?
What is the resident-to-caregiver ratio for this specific care level?
What is staff turnover?
How quickly are call buttons expected to be answered?
How are falls handled and documented?
How are emergencies, severe weather, power loss and evacuation handled?
Can we review state inspection / licensing information?

11. Memory care — ask now, not later

How much more does memory care cost than assisted living?
What is included in the memory-care price?
What additional fees can still be added?
Is memory care in the same building / campus?
Who determines when a resident needs memory care?
What dementia-specific training do staff receive?
How are wandering, agitation, nighttime confusion and refusal of care handled?
What behaviors or medical needs can memory care NOT manage?
Could the resident ever be required to move out? Under what circumstances?

12. The money questions people forget

How much have rates increased in each of the last 3 years?
How much notice is given before a rate increase?
What happens financially during hospitalization or rehab?
What happens if the resident runs out of money?
Does the community accept Medicaid now or later?
Does it accept long-term-care insurance? Will staff help with claim paperwork?
What deposit is required to hold a room?
What is the cancellation / move-out notice requirement?
