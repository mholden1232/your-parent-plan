How it works

One place. A clear order. Less scrambling.

Three steps, at your own pace

You don’t have to do it all at once. Start with what you already have, and the plan points you to what’s next.

  1. Bring in what you already have

    Scan or upload important documents and information instead of retyping everything.

  2. See what’s missing

    The preparedness checklist shows what has been handled and what still needs attention.

  3. Work the priorities

    “Your Next 3 Things” is based on incomplete checklist items and missing Core Records.

What this is, and isn’t

Your Parent Plan is a preparedness and organization tool. It is not a medical-care tracker.

It helps with: legal documents, insurance, parent income and resources, important contacts, medications for reference, future living options, facility comparison and family planning.
It does not: track doses taken, manage medication schedules, monitor symptoms, replace a medical portal, or provide individualized legal or financial advice.
