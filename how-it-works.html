What this is — and isn't

Your Parent Plan is a preparedness and organization tool. It is not a medical-care tracker.

It helps with:
Legal documents, insurance, parent income/resources, important contacts, medications for reference, future living options, facility comparison and family planning.
It does not:
Track doses taken, manage medication schedules, monitor symptoms, replace a medical portal, or provide individualized legal/financial advice.
Your Parent Plan is an organizational tool. It isn’t legal, medical, tax, or financial advice.
