Help

Common questions

Who can see my information?
Only you, and family members you invite. Each account’s plan, documents and vault are protected in the database so other accounts can’t read them.
How do I share the plan with my brother or sister?
Go to Account & family, enter their email and choose “Can edit” or “View only.” Copy the invitation link and send it to them. They create a free account with that same email and accept. Sharing is part of Plus; the people you invite don’t need to pay.
Is the Password Vault shared with family?
No. The vault is always personal. Entries are encrypted in your browser with a passphrase only you know, before they’re saved.
I forgot my vault passphrase.
It can’t be recovered, because it’s never stored. On the vault page, open “Forgot your vault passphrase?” to reset the vault. That deletes the saved passwords so you can start again.
I forgot my account password.
Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page and we’ll email you a reset link.
What’s the difference between Free and Plus?
Free covers the checklist, Essentials, the Money Snapshot, up to 2 facilities and 10 documents. Plus adds the Password Vault, family sharing, unlimited facilities and documents, savings projections, and next steps picked from your whole plan. See Pricing.
How do I cancel Plus?
Go to Account & family and choose Manage billing. Plus stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for, and your information stays in your account.
How do I delete my account?
At the bottom of Account & family. It permanently deletes your plan, documents, vault and sharing, and cancels Plus.
Is this legal, medical or financial advice?
No. Your Parent Plan helps you organize information and see what’s missing. For decisions, talk with an attorney, doctor or financial professional.
