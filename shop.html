Bathroom Safety Kit

Everything a parent’s bathroom usually needs, chosen together so you don’t have to guess.

  • Two 12-inch textured grab bars, one beside the toilet and one for the shower or tub
  • Non-slip shower strips for the tub or shower floor
  • Your choice of seating: a shower chair or a tub transfer bench
  • Raised toilet seat with handles
  • Plug-in motion night lights for the path from bed to bathroom

Which seating do they need?

If your parent steps into a walk-in shower, choose the shower chair. If they have to step over the side of a bathtub, choose the tub transfer bench: they sit down outside the tub and slide across.

Good to know

Who installs grab bars?
Grab bars have to be anchored into wall studs or proper backing to hold body weight. We recommend a qualified handyman or contractor.
Will insurance help pay?
Many of these items may be HSA/FSA eligible. Some Medicare Advantage plans also cover bathroom safety devices, so check your parent’s plan benefits first.
Not sure what fits?
Take the free Home Safety Checklist, or talk with your parent’s doctor or an occupational therapist about their specific needs. Check each item’s weight capacity against your parent’s needs.
